## Decision

> D07: Initial output is H.264 MP4 in 720p and 1080p.

(Amended by: PR-25a (D47.7). A single frame of the grid is also an output, as
PNG (the captured bytes), JPEG, or WebP, at the composition's size. Video output
stays H.264 MP4 in the presets `720p` and `1080p`; it is now rendered in
segments that a join puts together (D47.1 to D47.5).)
